1 Overview & Scope

Welcome to 304 Arena ("the App"), developed by Thamara Senanayake ("we", "us", or "our"). 304 Arena provides a South Asian classic trick-taking card game experience featuring offline solo bot play, real-time online multiplayer private rooms, and authentic regional variations (including Sri Lankan Requesting Trump and Face-Down Guessing).

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our application on Android devices, and the choices you have associated with that data under applicable data privacy regulations and Google Play Store policies.

Summary: We do not sell your personal data. We do not track your location, access your contacts, or read your device storage. We only collect the minimal information needed to operate multiplayer matchmaking, seat assignments, and gameplay synchronization.

2 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum amount of data required to provide a fair, stable, and seamless card game experience:

A. Information You Provide

  • Player Username / Nickname: When you open the application, you can set a display name (e.g. "Kasun_304"). This username is used strictly to identify your seat at the card table, distinguish your turns, and display scores on the table badge and lobby.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Anonymous Authentication Identifier: The app utilizes Firebase Authentication to generate an anonymous session UID. This identifier prevents duplicate seating in multiplayer rooms, allows reconnecting to active games if your connection drops, and preserves your username without requiring passwords or social logins.
  • Multiplayer Game State: When participating in private rooms or matchmaking, temporary game actions (such as card plays, bids, room codes, trick history, and rule mode selection) are temporarily recorded in Google Cloud Firestore to synchronize game moves across players in real-time.

C. Information We NEVER Collect

  • ❌ No Financial Information: The game is free-to-play. We do not request credit cards, bank accounts, or digital wallet details. There is no real-money gambling in 304 Arena.
  • ❌ No Sensitive Hardware Access: We do not access your Camera, Microphone, Photo Library, Contacts, SMS, or Call Logs.
  • ❌ No Precise Location: We do not collect GPS coordinates or fine location data.
  • ❌ No Advertising Trackers: We do not integrate advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral profiling trackers.

5 Data Retention & Account Deletion

Google Play Data Deletion Compliance: In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy on Account and Data Deletion, we provide complete control over your data.

A. Temporary Game Data Retention

Multiplayer room sessions and associated trick records stored in Cloud Firestore are temporary and automatically pruned or deleted once the game round finishes or when rooms become inactive.

B. How to Request Data Deletion

Since 304 Arena uses anonymous authentication, you can delete your profile and all associated data at any time through either of the following methods:

  1. Self-Service Device Reset: On your Android device, go to Settings > Apps > 304 Arena > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data. This immediately removes your local authentication token, username, and cached game settings from your device.
  2. Manual Deletion Request by Email: If you wish to ensure that any associated Cloud Firestore records or reserved usernames tied to your player name are permanently removed from our cloud servers, please email us with your player name. We will process and verify your request within 7 business days.

6 Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with your information. 304 Arena implements modern security measures:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmissions between the application and Google Cloud/Firebase servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).
  • Access Restrictions: Access to Firestore databases is governed by strict Cloud Firestore Security Rules, preventing unauthorized users from tampering with other players' seats or private rooms.
  • No Sensitive Data Storage: Because passwords, payment details, and personal contact info are never collected, the risk of sensitive credential exposure is minimized.

7 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

304 Arena is a traditional strategy card game designed for a general audience. The game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personally identifiable data, please contact us immediately. We will promptly take steps to delete any such information from our records.
